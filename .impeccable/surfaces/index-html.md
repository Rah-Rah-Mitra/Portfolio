# Portfolio surface brief

- Scope and mode: the single-page recruiter portfolio at `index.html`; Experience mode with a fast Persuade opening and an explicit camera-guided route through deeper evidence.
- Audience and job: recruiters should understand Rahul's connected intelligent-systems position, representative proof, suitable résumé, and contact path in roughly 20 seconds. Technical visitors can move deliberately between full chapters, filter the factual record, and open the optional assistant, Effects Lab, or Spatial Map.
- Outcome and proof: shorten the default page without removing evidence. Keep one selected project, capability, proof item, or résumé in the primary view at a time; preserve every selector, filter, archive, link, document, and truthful record as an immediately labeled control or progressive disclosure.
- Chosen direction: extend the established field notebook into a calibrated multi-camera survey. Each chapter is a distinct observation field with its own tinted paper, technical texture, frame label, and camera pose. A persistent journey rail connects Overview → Work → Capabilities → Journey → Proof → Résumés → Contact.
- Structural thesis: the page behaves like a sparse reconstruction assembled from decisive viewpoints, not one endless ledger. Selected Work becomes a camera deck with one active case study; capabilities become six observation markers with one active detail; field notes and proof use bounded default sets; résumés use a role selector with one active download surface; the full archives remain available on demand.
- Signature interaction and motion: clicking or scrolling between journey markers moves the active camera/frustum along a pose path. Chapter headings and active-view panels slide in once with restrained parallax and 320–520 ms transitions. Motion never gates content, never loops around reading copy, and collapses to static state under reduced motion.
- Geometry authority: camera frustums, sparse points, match-graph edges, outside-looking-in trajectories, and pose labels follow Structure-from-Motion conventions described by COLMAP, Building Rome in a Day, Tanks and Temples, and ScanNet++. No external dataset is bundled and no visual motif is presented as Rahul's completed research.
- Boundaries: preserve factual content, Build/Secure lens, project and event filters, archive search, private API and fallback, analytics, all effects, Three.js/GLB assets, résumé downloads, accessibility, and canonical SEO. Do not copy the reference site's palette, typography, copy, card shapes, or imagery; do not imply unsupported robotics, SLAM, localization, mapping, or Gaussian-splatting delivery.
- States and adaptation: desktop uses a vertical camera rail and wide active-view stages; tablet uses a compact stage strip; mobile uses a horizontally scrollable chapter navigator and stacked active views. Critical information remains visible with JavaScript, animation, or optional effects unavailable.
