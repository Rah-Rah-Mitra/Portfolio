asset library

Every cell below is a working mechanism, not a picture of one. One algebra file carries the library — points, lines, join, meet, rotors, translators, motors — and the sandwich product M p M̃ does all placement. Nothing is keyframed: each part is placed by a motor and its dependents are solved from constraints every frame (circle ∩ circle, line ∩ circle, foot of a perpendicular, mesh phase).

Drive method is printed beside each asset. Nothing is wired into the desktop or phone environments yet — this is the parts bin.
