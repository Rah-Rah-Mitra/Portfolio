Licence for the Estate model data (public/estate/**)
====================================================

The files under /estate/ on this site (the folder public/estate/ in the
portfolio repository) are model data for Sample Town N5, a generated sample
HDB neighbourhood: the poster render, the building, site and interior
geometry, the walk grids and the navigation data the Estate window loads.
They are not a real town and not HDB's own plans.

Source and changes
------------------

The data was generated by Bonsai-Estate (IfcOpenShell, Bonsai and Blender)
and published in the model and reports zips of its GitHub release v1.2 (the
poster render and its camera views come from the reports zip, everything
else from the model zip):

  https://github.com/Rah-Rah-Mitra/Bonsai-Estate/releases/tag/v1.2

For the web, the portfolio's pack tool (scripts/estate in the portfolio
repository) converted that release: geometry merged by storey and
compressed (meshopt), materials mapped onto the site's design tokens,
passable door leaves shown open, files renamed by content hash and gzipped.
It also simplified what is drawn: lift cars dropped; ground faces facing
down turned up, and the undersides of ground slabs below grade dropped;
window glass drawn as panels with a back face and shelter glass as an
opaque tint; furniture and trees beyond a short distance drawn as
simplified stand-ins; the poster resized and re-encoded. The
pack.<hash>.json manifest in the version folder records the exact release
assets (SHA-256), the commit they were built from and the corrections it
made (its warnings). No modelled fact (a room, a storey, a door or a
dimension) was added.

If you reuse the data, credit it as above, link the licence and the
repository, and say whether you changed it.
